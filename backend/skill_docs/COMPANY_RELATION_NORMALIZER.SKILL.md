# Company Relation Normalizer

Application governance SOP, version 1.0.0.

Normalize source-reported ownership and classification with evidence and dates.
Ownership is an observation as of a disclosed report date; do not assert unchanged
current ownership. Preserve shares, units, percentage denominator and nominee roles.
Do not infer control from a largest-shareholder label or a common nominee.
Company industry and security size/liquidity are different classification scopes.
Model or uncertain text extraction always produces a pending candidate.
Only a separately audited, explicitly selected source-validation rule may accept
literal structured observations. Never auto-accept inferred legal or supply facts.
Corrections append evidence and review history; do not overwrite old assertions.

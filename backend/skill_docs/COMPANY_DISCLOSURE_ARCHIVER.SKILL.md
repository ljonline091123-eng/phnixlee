# Company Disclosure Archiver

Application governance SOP, version 1.0.0.

Archive the actual response, publication and retrieval times, request parameters,
publisher, transport provider, URL and content hash. Preserve PDF page text and
binary hash. Repeated content must not create a new version solely due to a fetch.
An inaccessible PDF or title-only result is incomplete evidence, not a parsed case.
An issuer announcing litigation is not necessarily a defendant or even a party.
Distinguish subsidiaries, courts, party roles, appeal and enforcement proceedings.
Never infer guilt, loss, beneficial ownership or absence of litigation.
Official registry and court services without authorization remain AUTH_REQUIRED.

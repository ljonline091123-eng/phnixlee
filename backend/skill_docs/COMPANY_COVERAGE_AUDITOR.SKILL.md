# Company Coverage Auditor

Application governance SOP, version 1.0.0.

For every requested stock report whether its company mapping exists, its identity
basis, retrieved sources, evidence, fact statuses and known data gaps.
Separate failure, empty search, authentication required, unsupported market and
not requested. No record is not proof of no relationship or no litigation.
Preserve sample members without successful mapping in the coverage denominator.
Publish bounded stock/company graphs with explicit truncation and source paths.
Research consumes only accepted, time-applicable facts and keeps provenance.
Report exact changes and confirm legacy rows remain unchanged against a backup.
